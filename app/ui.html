    Friends

    Friends see what you watch and can invite you to their party, right in F1 Party.

      No friends yet. Click the green button and send the link, or add someone in a party with the add-friend icon next to their name.

      Teams

      A team is a group of friends with one Discord channel: anyone in it can post party invites there. A party is one session you watch together, with a 5-letter code.

      In Discord: Server settings → Integrations → Webhooks → New webhook, pick the channel, Copy webhook URL. It's kept safe on the F1 Party server, never on anyone's computer.

        No teams yet. Start one with the + button, or paste a team link a friend sent you.
